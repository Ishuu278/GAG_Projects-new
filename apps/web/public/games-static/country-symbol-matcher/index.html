How to Play ❄️

  • Match a Country flag to its corresponding National Symbol.
  • The Path: You can only connect two tiles if a clear, unblocked path can be drawn between them through empty spaces.
  • Turns: The connecting path can have a maximum of 2 turns (up to 3 straight lines).
  • Edges: Paths are allowed to travel outside the outer boundary of the board.
